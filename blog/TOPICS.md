# Blog topic queue

The weekly job picks the **topmost unused topic**, writes it, then moves the
line to "Published" with the date. Keep the queue stocked — if it empties, the
job will propose new topics rather than repeat old ones.

Three kinds of post, in order of preference:

1. **SOP** — a procedure a shop can actually follow, using our products. These
   are the most valuable: they answer "how do I do this", they are naturally
   quotable by answer engines, and nobody else in Indian refractories publishes
   them properly.
2. **Diagnostic** — "why is X happening and what to check". Targets the way
   buyers actually search when something has gone wrong.
3. **Industry note** — developments that genuinely affect our customers.

## Rules for industry notes

These carry more risk than SOPs because they make claims about the outside
world rather than about our own products.

- Every factual claim needs a source that can be linked. No source, no claim.
- Never state a competitor's specifications, pricing or performance.
- Do not predict prices or market movements.
- If the week's news cannot be verified, **write an SOP instead**. An unsourced
  industry note is worse than no post.
