Current Research Themes

Present research work (2016 - 2025).

Casting Powder / Mold Flux

  • Fluorine free designs and peritectic grade casting stability.
  • High Al and Ti bearing steel interactions with flux composition.
  • Crystallization control to balance lubrication and heat transfer.

See 2018 to 2025 mold flux studies in the library above for examples.

Nozzle Filling Compound

  • Optimization of chromite silica ratio to control sintering.
  • Engineered particle structures to tune free opening behavior.
  • Correlations between PSD, composition, and lancing frequency.

Recent studies emphasize sintering windows and free opening reliability.

Castable

  • No cement binder systems and improved slag resistance.
  • Colloidal silica sol gel binders for strength and setting control.
  • Geopolymer binders as low cement alternatives.

Current work centers on binder chemistry and durability under slag attack.

Ladle Covering Compound

  • Insulation powders that reduce reoxidation in tundish practice.
  • Flux chemistry effects on steel cleanliness and refractory wear.
  • Tradeoffs between thermal insulation and inclusion control.

Recent papers compare flux types and their impact on reoxidation.

Future Scope

Inference based on recent literature trends.

Casting Powder / Mold Flux

  • Broader shift to low emission and fluorine free flux systems.
  • Data driven design of viscosity and crystallization windows.
  • In situ monitoring of slag film and heat transfer for control.

Inference based on 2018 to 2025 mold flux studies listed above.

Nozzle Filling Compound

  • Engineered particle architectures to control sintering onset.
  • Predictive models linking composition to free opening rates.
  • Lower contamination and more consistent filler sand batches.

Inference based on filler sand studies in 2012 to 2022.

Castable

  • Broader adoption of cement free and geopolymer binders.
  • Improved thermal shock resistance with lower carbon systems.
  • Faster installation methods with consistent curing behavior.

Inference based on 2020 to 2024 castable binder research.

Ladle Covering Compound

  • Hybrid cover powders targeting both insulation and cleanliness.
  • Flux chemistries tuned to reduce refractory degradation.
  • Integration with shrouding and ladle lid practices.

Inference based on 2017 to 2025 tundish flux studies.
