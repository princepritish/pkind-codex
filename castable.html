Performance Overview

PK INDUSTRIES castable is suited for high temperature and wear-sensitive furnace regions. It supports monolithic lining construction and improved structural integrity.

Compared with conventional refractory brick assemblies, castable can improve lining continuity, reduce joint-related failures, and lower heat loss in selected applications.

FAQ

Castable Questions

Which grade should we use?

Grade selection depends on temperature profile, abrasion level, and thermal shock condition.

Can this reduce maintenance shutdowns?

Proper installation and grade selection can improve lining life and reduce frequent patching.

Do you guide water addition and curing?

Yes. Basic installation and curing guidance is provided with supply for better field performance.
