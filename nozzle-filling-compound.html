Performance Overview

Nozzle filling compound is a critical safety and quality element in ladle operation. Our blend is designed to avoid sintering while ensuring stable free opening performance.

Material selection and particle engineering are adjusted for heat size, bore configuration, and steel grade behavior to reduce process disruptions.
