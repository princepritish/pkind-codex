FAQs

Contacting us

How quickly does P.K. Industries respond to enquiries?

WhatsApp enquiries typically receive a response within 2 hours during working hours. Email enquiries are usually answered within one working day. Office hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Include your steel grades, casting route and speed, mould or ladle dimensions, current consumption figures if known, and the specific problem you are trying to solve. That is enough to propose a starting grade rather than a generic datasheet.

Do you supply trial quantities?

Yes. Trial quantities are supplied with technical usage guidance, and the formulation is revised based on measured performance during the trial heats.
