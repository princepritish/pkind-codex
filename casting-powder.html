Low-consumption casting powder designed for no blackouts, brighter billets, and near-zero slag carryover, while maintaining stable mould lubrication and thermal control.

Performance Overview

PK INDUSTRIES Casting Powder contains low melting constituents that quickly produce liquid slag. This slag penetrates the mould-shell gap and improves lubrication and thermal control.

We supply formulations for billet, bloom, and slab casting routes. Grades can be customized for casting speed, steel chemistry, and mould dimensions.
