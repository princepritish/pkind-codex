What Plant Teams Usually Need

Consumables are selected around process problems, not only product names.

Continuous Casting Requirements

In continuous casting, the right Casting Powder helps create a stable slag layer, supports lubrication between the shell and mould, and contributes to controlled heat transfer. A plant facing breakouts, surface defects, unstable powder consumption, or inconsistent billet brightness should not select a grade only from a generic name. The starting point should include casting speed, section size, steel chemistry, mould dimensions, and the existing powder behavior observed by the operator.

PK INDUSTRIES uses these details to suggest a practical trial grade for billet, bloom, slab, round, and open casting practices. The aim is to make the first trial meaningful, so plant teams can compare performance against their current consumable instead of running a blind replacement.

Ladle and Refractory Maintenance Requirements

Ladle opening, refractory repair, and molten steel heat retention are closely connected to shop stability. A Nozzle Filling Compound should support free opening without excessive lancing. A Castable should match the application zone, temperature, abrasion, curing time, and shutdown window. A Ladle Covering Compound should spread quickly and reduce temperature loss during holding and transfer.

For this reason, our enquiry process asks for the plant issue first: lancing frequency, erosion area, holding time, target temperature drop, repair location, and required dispatch timeline. This helps our team recommend the right product family instead of simply quoting a standard material.
