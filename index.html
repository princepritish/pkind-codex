What we manufacture

Four consumables that decide how cleanly a heat casts

P.K. Industries manufactures four categories of steel melting shop consumables: casting powder (continuous casting mould flux), nozzle filling compound for ladle slide gate systems, refractory castable for furnace and ladle linings, and ladle covering compound for molten steel temperature retention. Each is supplied in grade variants matched to the customer's casting route and operating conditions.

Casting Powder

Mould flux that melts to a stable liquid slag film, lubricating the mould–shell gap and controlling horizontal heat transfer. Supplied for billet, bloom and slab routes.

Specifications

Nozzle Filling Compound

Chromite and silica based free-opening compound that resists sintering at ladle temperatures, so the slide gate opens on demand without oxygen lancing.

Specifications

Refractory Castable

Dense and insulating castables for wear-prone furnace zones, ladle linings and launders, with controlled installation and heat-up behaviour.

Specifications

Ladle Covering Compound

Low-density insulating cover that spreads across the ladle surface to cut radiative heat loss and shield the melt from reoxidation during holding and casting.

How we work

From requirement mapping to a stable running grade

01

Requirement mapping

We capture steel grades, casting speed, mould size, ladle size, tundish practice and the specific problem you are solving.

02

Grade selection

A starting formulation is proposed from that data — basicity, melting range and carbon content chosen for your conditions.

03

Controlled production

Raw materials are checked on intake, blended to fixed batch parameters, and released against defined criteria before packing.

04

Trial and feedback

We track performance through the trial heats and revise the formulation until behaviour is stable and repeatable.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What does P.K. Industries manufacture?

Four categories of steel melting shop consumables: casting powder (continuous casting mould flux), nozzle filling compound for ladle slide gate systems, refractory castable for furnace and ladle linings, and ladle covering compound (Radex) for molten steel temperature retention.

Where is P.K. Industries located?

At C 10, Near Industrial Estate, Adityapur, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand 832109, India — inside the Jamshedpur steel cluster, which shortens delivery lead times to steel producers across eastern India.

Do you provide plant-specific trial grades?

Yes. We recommend trial grades based on steel grade, casting route, casting speed and mould dimensions. Trial quantities are supplied with technical usage guidance, and formulations are adjusted from measured plant performance feedback.

Can casting powder be customised for casting speed and heat profile?

Yes. Chemistry, basicity, melting range and carbon content are tuned to casting speed, steel chemistry and mould size. Higher casting speeds generally require lower viscosity and faster melting behaviour to keep a stable liquid slag film in the mould–shell gap.

How long has P.K. Industries been in business?

The company was established in 2001 and has supplied refractory consumables to steel melting shops for over two decades. It is owned and managed by Mr. P.K. Gupta.
