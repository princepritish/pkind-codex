About P.K. Industries

Focused on clean casting, temperature retention, and safer operations.

Introduction

P.K. Industries is a well-known manufacturer, exporter, and supplier. We offer our clients high-quality nozzle filling compound, castable, casting powder, and radex insulation powder.

P.K. Industries is situated in Jharkhand (India). The company is run and managed by our mentor and guide, Mr. P.K. Gupta (Owner). We are soaring to new heights of success with each passing day.
