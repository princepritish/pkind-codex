About the company

Refractory consumables, made in the Jamshedpur steel cluster since 2001

P.K. Industries manufactures casting powder, nozzle filling compound, refractory castable and ladle covering compound for steel melting shops and continuous casting operations.

P.K. Industries is a manufacturer, exporter and supplier of steel melting shop consumables, established in 2001 and based at Adityapur, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand. The company is owned and managed by Mr. P.K. Gupta and supplies casting powder, nozzle filling compound, refractory castable and ladle covering compound to steel producers across India.

Why location matters in this business

Adityapur sits inside one of the densest steel manufacturing regions in India. For a consumables supplier that is not a marketing detail — it changes what kind of service is possible.

Refractory consumables are not commodities that perform identically everywhere. A casting powder that runs beautifully at one plant can cause blackouts at another with a different casting speed, mould size or steel grade mix. The only reliable way to close that gap is to watch the material run and adjust it. Being an hour from a customer's shop floor rather than a day makes that iterative loop practical.

It also means shorter delivery lead times to producers across Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha, Chhattisgarh and Bihar — useful when a shop needs a top-up before a scheduled campaign.

How we work with a new plant

We start by mapping your conditions rather than sending a datasheet: steel grades, casting route and speed, mould and ladle dimensions, tundish practice, holding times, and the specific symptom you want to fix.

From that we propose a starting formulation and supply a trial quantity with usage guidance. We then track how it behaves through the trial heats and revise the formulation until the behaviour is stable and repeatable. Most grades that end up running well are the second or third iteration, not the first.

Production discipline

Raw materials are checked on intake. Blending runs to fixed batch parameters so that consistency between batches is a controlled property rather than an accident. Material is released against defined criteria before it is packed and dispatched.

We are direct about what this is: it is a disciplined process operated by a small team who know the material. Where a customer's procurement process requires formal third-party certification, tell us at enquiry stage so we can be clear about what we can and cannot document.

What we make

  • Casting powder — continuous casting mould flux for billet, bloom and slab routes
  • Nozzle filling compound — free-opening compound for ladle slide gate systems
  • Refractory castable — 60% and 90% alumina grades for monolithic linings
  • Ladle covering compound (Radex) — insulating cover for molten steel temperature retention
